Features

The app records what happened at the table — it never rolls or decides for you.

Roster & warband tracker

Heroes, henchmen groups and hired swords with full statlines, XP tracks, skills, injuries, equipment and photos. Twenty-two warband types.

Trading Post

Buy common and rare gear against your gold, roll for availability or resolve by hand, and sell back from the treasury. Prices from the rulebook.

Shared campaigns

A join code drops your group into one campaign — standings, a shared battle log, awards and rivalries, and game nights on a calendar.

Offline rules reference

The full core rulebook, searchable and grouped by chapter, plus skills, scenarios and warband rules — with a dice roller for the table.

Printable roster sheet

The classic Mordheim roster, laid out from your data with the Experience boxes already ticked. Print it, or save it as a PDF.

Pre- & during-battle

Pick or roll a scenario, note the opponent, then track the turn, casualties and a timestamped event log — surviving a refresh mid-game.
